OUR FOUNDATION

Welcome To Cap Resorts

Founded in 2000, Cap Resorts creates memorable hospitality experiences that connect travellers with nature, comfort, and exceptional service across India.

From secluded coastal sanctuaries to verdant mountain pavilions, Cap Resorts is engineered for those who seek uncompromised luxury without disturbing the fragile beauty of the natural environment. Each property reflects local architectural traditions, enriched with modern 5-star amenities, Michelin-inspired dining, and holistic wellness centers.
